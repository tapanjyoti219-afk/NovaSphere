NovaSphere

Your Future. Your Opportunity.

NovaSphere is a jobs and scholarships information platform focused on Assam and all India.

Our goal is to make opportunities easier to discover through a simple, reliable, and mobile-friendly platform.

🎯 Our Mission

To help students, job seekers, and individuals discover relevant opportunities with clear eligibility details and official application links.

🚀 Planned Features

Jobs

- Assam Government Jobs
- Central Government Jobs
- Police & Defence Recruitment
- Railway & Banking Jobs
- Private Jobs
- ITI & Fresher Jobs

Scholarships

- School, College, ITI and Diploma Scholarships
- Graduation and Post-Graduation Scholarships
- Merit-based and Need-based Scholarships
- Assam and All-India Scholarships

Platform Features

- Search and advanced filters
- Saved Jobs and Scholarships
- User accounts and profiles
- Personalized opportunity recommendations
- Notifications and deadline reminders
- Daily source-checking system
- Offline access to saved opportunities
- Low-data mode for mobile users

📱 Built for Everyone

NovaSphere is planned as a mobile-first, responsive web application with PWA support for ordinary Android devices.

🔒 Trusted Information

We aim to provide official notification links, application links, eligibility details, and last-verified information wherever available.

Users should always confirm eligibility and deadlines through the official notification.

🛠️ Planned Technology

- GitHub
- Cloudflare Pages
- Supabase
- GitHub Actions
- Progressive Web App (PWA)

Technology choices and integrations will be finalized during development.
